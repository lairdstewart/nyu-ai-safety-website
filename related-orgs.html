Related Organizations in NYC

A collection of related organizations. Inclusion does not imply an affiliation with the association.

Substantial AI Safety programming
  • NYC AI Governance and Safety. "fosters convening spaces for professionals working across AI safety, security, governance, and public policy"
  • Collider. Cowork and collaboration space for the AI safety community
  • Effective Altriuism NYC. Has a subgroup on AI.
  • Anthropic has open "trust and safety" roles, but not alignment (only SF/London)
  • OpenAI. Most safety roles in SF, one open data scientist safety role for NYC.
  • Columbia AI Alignment Club. Columbia's student-led AI safety club.
Adjacent orgs
  • ai@nyu. Website highlighting AI research, events, and announcements.
  • AI Now Institute. Independent AI policy research institute with a "Safety & Security" research area.
  • NYU EA
  • Center for responsible AI. Research center focusing on "responsible data engineering, algorithmic fairness, transparency and explainability, and privacy and data protection".
  • Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy. NYU center which seeks to "advance understanding of the nature and value of nonhuman minds, with special focus on animals and AI"
  • Center for Mind, Brain, and Consciousness. "Devoted to foundational issues in the mind-brain sciences." Has hosted computational conciousness and AI welfare programming.
  • Ethical Tech Colab. Collaboration between NYU's Center for Global Affairs and Microsoft. Research agenda includes using AI to improve emergency and diplomatic decision making.
