About us

The NYU AI Safety Association is a student-led club which advances AI safety research and helps its members pursue careers in the field. Students from all departments are welcome to join!

To get involved, sign up for our email list, connect with us on LinkedIn or reach out directly with questions, suggestions, or simply to say hi!
