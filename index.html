About

The NYU AI Safety Association is a student-led club which

  • Examines risks posed by powerful AI systems
  • Facilitates study of interpretability and alignment research
  • Supports members pursuing careers in AI Safety

The association is currently being founded (08/2026). If you'd like to help, please email exec@nyuaisafety.org.
