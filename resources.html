Resources

A collection of AI Safety related organizations and people. Inclusion does not imply a relationship with NYU AI Safety. This list is not yet complete.

Related organizations at NYU

  • NYU Alignment Research Group. PI Sam Bowman is on leave.
  • NYU Effective Altruism. Previously held a retreat on AI Safety.
  • Center for Responsible AI. Research center focusing on "responsible data engineering, algorithmic fairness, transparency and explainability, and privacy and data protection". Has researched preventing LLM jailbreaks.
  • petal. Student club for policy, ethics, tech, accountability, law.

Related organizations in NYC

  • Collider. New York AI safety community
  • Columbia AI Alignment Club

NYU Professors with related research

  • He He. Interpretability, evaluation, and oversight.
  • Kyunghyun Cho. Mainly focuses on AI for biology and medicine, but has published on alignment, unlearning, and interpretability.
  • Joan Bruna. Mostly deep learning theory. One interpretability paper.
  • Eunsol Choi. Partial focus on continual learning. Papers on LLM behavioral evaluation and implicit user feedback for training.
  • Greg Durrett. NLP and machine learning. Many papers on alignment and interpretability.
